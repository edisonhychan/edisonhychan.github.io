About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Electronic and Computer Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).


I am particularly interested in applied AI and machine learning, robotics, sensing systems, and edge intelligence. I enjoy building systems that connect software, algorithms, and physical hardware rather than treating them as separate components.


Currently, I am exploring projects involving medical sensing, automated experimentation, robot learning, and real-world AI deployment.

Selected Projects

Automated Spirometry Calibration Platform

Developing an automated experimental platform for studying and correcting volume underestimation in spirometry measurements at high airflow rates. The system combines motion control, sensing, automated data collection, and machine-learning-based calibration.
